Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

Your privacy is critically important to us. At FastImageTool, we have a few fundamental principles:

  • We don’t ask you for personal information unless we truly need it.
  • We don’t share your personal information with anyone except to comply with the law, develop our products, or protect our rights.
  • We don’t store personal information on our servers unless required for the on‑going operation of one of our services.

Information We Collect

No image uploads to FastImageTool: Conversion and compression run in the tab. Product photos are not posted to our origin. Ads and analytics scripts still load — see Cookies and advertising below.

Analytics: We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data such as pages viewed, device type, and general location. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and advertising: This site uses Google AdSense and Google Analytics (GA4). A first-party banner records Accept or Reject and updates Consent Mode (defaults: denied). That banner is not a Google-certified IAB TCF CMP. Visitors in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland may see limited ads unless a Google-certified consent message is active for this site. How Google uses data: partner sites; ads cookies: here.

On-site search: The homepage search box filters a first-party JSON index (/search.json) in your browser. It does not send query text to a third-party search API.

Log data: Like most websites, we collect limited log data (browser type, referring pages, date/time) to help us understand how the site is used. This data is anonymized.

What we do not collect

We do not create accounts. We do not receive the images you edit. Do not email us unpublished catalog photos or ID documents.

Third‑Party Links

Our site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We encourage you to review it periodically.
