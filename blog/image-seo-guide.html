Image filenames and alt text (what we can and cannot do)

FastImageTool does not rank your Meesho or Amazon listing. Search engines look at your store page, title, price, and reviews. What we can do is make the file smaller and easier to name before you upload it.

What actually matters on a listing photo

  1. Pixels the marketplace asked for. Meesho square 600×600; Amazon India 1000px+ on the long side. See the seller guides.
  2. File size under the upload cap so the form does not fail on mobile. Use Image Compressor. JPEG at ~85% is lossy — it is smaller, not “lossless.”
  3. A filename a human can read after download, e.g. kurta-maroon-600.jpg, not IMG_4401.jpg. Rename on disk. We do not auto-rename SKUs.

Alt text is on your site, not here

If you put photos on Shopify or a blog, write alt text in that CMS. Describe the product (“maroon cotton kurta, front view”). Do not stuff keywords. FastImageTool does not write alt text into the image file.
