We do not remove image backgrounds — what sellers should do instead

FastImageTool does not include a background-removal tool. We will not pretend otherwise. There is no magic “cut out the product” button here. This page exists so you are not sent looking for a feature we do not ship.

What we actually offer

Our tools resize, compress, convert formats, crop from the center, rotate in 90° steps, and apply simple filters (blur, sharpen, brightness). They run in your browser. They do not detect a subject or erase a messy room behind a kurta.

How Meesho and Amazon sellers usually get a clean background

  1. Shoot it clean. White paper roll, collapsible backdrop, or a well-lit wall. Amazon India’s main image expects a white field (RGB 255,255,255). Lighting does more than any editor.
  2. Crop tight. Use Image Crop to keep the product in a square (for example 600×600) by entering width and height. Crop is from the center — frame the product first on your phone if needed.
  3. Do not fake transparency from a JPEG. Converting JPG to PNG does not add a transparent background. The JPEG already discarded alpha.
  4. Blur only hides, it does not cut. Image Blur softens the whole frame. It is not a subject mask.

If you truly need a cut-out

Use a dedicated background-removal app or desktop editor (GIMP, Photoshop, or a specialist service). Then bring the PNG here to resize and compress for the marketplace upload cap (Meesho: stay under 2MB; confirm the live panel).

Why this article is on the blog

An older version of this URL described generic “free methods” as if FastImageTool did the job. That was misleading. We rewrote it so a reviewer — and a seller — can see the limit in one place.
