Contact FastImageTool

FastImageTool is a small, browser-based image toolkit for Meesho, Amazon India, and Shopify sellers. There is no account system and no upload server — if a tool fails, we need a description of the page and browser, not your product photos.

Email: toolsforgehq@gmail.com

We read mail in India Standard Time and typically reply within 1–2 business days. Weekend messages may wait until Monday.

What to email us about

  • Bugs: tool name, browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox), and what happened. Example: “Rotate Image on Chrome 128, 90° download produced a black PNG.”
  • Wrong copy: a page that claims a feature the tool does not have. We treat that as a defect.
  • New tool requests: one sentence on the marketplace rule you are trying to meet (for example “Flipkart needs 1200px on the long side”).
  • Legal / takedown: put “Takedown” in the subject. Include the URL and why the content should come down.
  • Affiliate / Amazon Associates: questions about the recommended gear page or our tag.

What we cannot do

  • We cannot edit, retouch, or batch-process images for you. Every tool runs locally in your browser — emailing a photo to us is unnecessary and we will not store it.
  • We cannot reset a Meesho, Amazon, or Shopify seller account. Contact those platforms directly.
  • We do not offer phone support or a live chat widget.
  • We do not sell software licenses or “premium unlocks.” The tools on this site are free.

Privacy of your message

Use email only for text. Do not attach unpublished catalog shots, ID documents, or customer data. See our privacy policy for how the website itself handles analytics and ads. Images you process on FastImageTool are not uploaded to our origin.
