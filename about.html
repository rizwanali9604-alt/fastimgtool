About FastImageTool

FastImageTool is a free, browser-based toolkit for Indian ecommerce sellers and small shops. It compresses, resizes, converts formats, and applies simple canvas filters. It is not Photoshop: no layers, no background removal, no freehand crop, no batch folder upload. Image processing stays in the browser (not posted to our origin). The site still loads analytics and ads scripts — details in the privacy policy.

Why We Built This

Indian sellers on Meesho, Amazon India, Flipkart, and Shopify face a constant challenge: every platform has different image requirements. Meesho catalog cards are square. A practical starting size many sellers use is 600×600 pixels, and files should stay under 2MB so mobile uploads do not fail. Live pixel and file-size caps appear in the Meesho Supplier Panel (Meesho’s public how-it-works page says detailed image guidelines are shown there). Amazon India typically wants at least 1000 pixels on the longest side. Shopify performs best with compressed WebP or JPEG, often under 500KB.

Most image tools are either expensive (₹500–₹2,000 per month), require you to upload your product photos to unknown servers, or are designed for professional photographers — not for a seller preparing 200 product listings before a Meesho flash sale deadline.

FastImageTool was built to solve this. It is free to use (supported by ads), runs in the browser, and is designed around the real requirements of Indian marketplace sellers.

What Makes FastImageTool Different

Browser-based image processing: Conversion and compression run in the tab. Product photos are not posted to FastImageTool. Ads and analytics scripts still load — see the privacy policy.

Built for Indian Sellers: Our guides reference actual Meesho, Amazon India, and Flipkart requirements with real pixel dimensions and file size limits updated for 2026.

Free to use: No subscription, no watermarks, no signup. FastImageTool is free for individuals, sellers, and businesses, and is supported by ads.

Single-file tools: Compress, resize, convert, and a few color filters. One image per visit. How-to copy lives on each tool page — we do not keep a duplicate “how to use X” article for every slider.
