About Image Resizer

Marketplace cards crop aggressively. Meesho sellers commonly target 600×600 square images; Amazon India favors large, sharp files with the product filling most of the frame. Phone gallery tools rarely offer exact pixel presets. This resizer can (1) lock aspect and scale, (2) unlock aspect and stretch to exact pixels, or (3) use a marketplace/social preset, which center-crops to fill the size so a 4:3 photo is not stretched into a square. Download is JPEG at quality 0.92. One file at a time. Processing stays in the browser.

How to Use Image Resizer

  1. Upload one image (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, or BMP that the browser can decode).
  2. For Meesho/Amazon/Instagram squares, click a preset — the preview shows a center crop, not a stretch.
  3. For a custom size that must keep proportions, leave “Lock aspect ratio” on and change one side.
  4. Download. The file name includes the output width and height.

Privacy: The image is not posted to FastImageTool servers. Analytics and ads scripts still load on the page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my product look squashed before?

Older versions of this page stretched presets with unlocked aspect. Presets now crop from the center to fill 600×600 / 1000×1000 / 1080×1080. Edges of a wide photo are cut, not distorted.

What sizes should Meesho and Amazon sellers use?

Meesho listings commonly use at least 600×600px. Amazon India typically needs 1000px or more on the longest side for zoom. Confirm current rules in Seller Central or your Meesho panel.
