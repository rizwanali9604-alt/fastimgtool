About PNG to JPG

Quality slider helps balance sharpness vs upload limits. Faster than re-exporting from a design suite on a slow PC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my transparent edges turn white?

JPEG cannot store transparency. This page fills the canvas with #FFFFFF, then draws the PNG. Checkerboard or colored backgrounds are not offered.

When should I use PNG to WebP instead?

When you want a smaller file and the destination supports WebP (Shopify, many sites). Use JPG when a marketplace or email client is picky.
