About JPG to PNG

Some design workflows and printers ask for PNG. JPG to PNG converts in the browser without uploading to a third-party converter. Note: converting JPG to PNG does not magically add transparency — the jpeg already discarded alpha. Use PNG when a workflow requires it; use JPG/WebP when you need smaller files for Meesho uploads.

How to Use JPG to PNG

  1. Step: Upload a JPEG file.
  2. Step: The tool converts it to PNG in your browser.
  3. Step: Click Download to save the PNG file.

Privacy: Processing is designed to run in your browser. Your images are not uploaded to a FastImageTool account.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is JPG to PNG?

JPG to PNG is a free format converter that runs in your browser. Upload JPEG, download PNG. Private and free.

Will PNG be smaller than JPG?

Usually larger. Convert for format compatibility, not for compression. Compress or use WebP when size is the goal.

Free?

Yes.

Transparency?

Source JPG has no transparency to preserve. Start from PNG/WebP with alpha if you need see-through backgrounds.
