About HEIC to JPG

iPhone default HEIC breaks many Windows uploaders and Seller Central forms. HEIC to JPG converts in the browser so sellers can move from Camera Roll to Meesho/Amazon without a Mac. Set JPEG quality, download, then resize to marketplace pixels.

How to Use HEIC to JPG

  1. Choose an iPhone .heic / .heif.
  2. Wait for in-browser conversion (heic2any, loaded from this site).
  3. Set JPEG quality (default 90%) and download.

Limitation: Firefox support is weaker than Chrome/Edge. Live Photos extras are not kept.
