How to use HEIC to JPG

  3. Step 3: Download JPG when conversion completes in your browser.

Practical tips

  • Keep a full-resolution master of every product photo; export sized copies for each sales channel.
  • For Meesho, aim for at least 600×600px and a manageable file size under 2MB.
  • For Amazon India main images, prefer accurate color on a white background and 1000px+ on the longest side.

Frequently asked questions

What does HEIC to JPG do?

HEIC to JPG is a free browser converter for Apple HEIC/HEIF photos into widely accepted JPEG files.

My browser fails to read HEIC — why?

Some browsers need adequate HEIC decoding support. Update the browser or try Chrome/Edge current versions. As a fallback, share from iPhone as Most Compatible before transfer.
