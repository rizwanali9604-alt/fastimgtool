Phones still save sideways photos when sensors disagree. Marketplaces reject or display tall products poorly when orientation is wrong. Rotate Image applies 90° / 180° / 270° turns in the browser so warehouse staff can fix folders of shots without Photoshop.

How to use Rotate Image

  1. Step 1: Upload your image.
  2. Step 2: Select rotation: 90° clockwise, 180°, or 90° counter-clockwise.
  3. Step 3: Click Download to save the rotated image.

Practical tips

  • Keep a full-resolution master of every product photo; export sized copies for each sales channel.
  • For Meesho, aim for at least 600×600px and a manageable file size under 2MB.
  • For Amazon India main images, prefer accurate color on a white background and 1000px+ on the longest side.
  • This guide describes only the controls that exist on the tool page — no hidden brush tools or paid accounts.
