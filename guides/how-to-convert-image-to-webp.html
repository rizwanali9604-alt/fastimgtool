Shopify and modern sites load faster with WebP. Image to WebP converts JPG/PNG with a quality slider in the browser so store owners shrink assets without a CLI. Pair with compressor workflows when you also need exact pixel sizes for banners.

How to use Image to WebP

  1. Step 1: Upload JPG or PNG.
  2. Step 2: Set WebP quality (10–100%).
  3. Step 3: Download the smaller WebP file for your website or Shopify store.

Practical tips

  • Keep a full-resolution master of every product photo; export sized copies for each sales channel.
  • For Meesho, aim for at least 600×600px and a manageable file size under 2MB.
  • For Amazon India main images, prefer accurate color on a white background and 1000px+ on the longest side.
  • This guide describes only the controls that exist on the tool page — no hidden brush tools or paid accounts.

Frequently asked questions

What does Image to WebP do?

Image to WebP is a free browser converter that outputs the WebP format for faster page loads. Upload JPG or PNG, set quality, download.

Do all marketplaces accept WebP?

Many Indian marketplaces still prefer JPG for listing images. Use WebP primarily for your own storefront or blog.

Quality tip?
