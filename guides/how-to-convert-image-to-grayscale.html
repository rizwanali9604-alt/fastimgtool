Black-and-white conversion is useful for documents, print drafts, artistic feeds, or reducing visual noise in instructional graphics. Image Grayscale converts in the browser without a subscription. Marketplace main images usually stay in color; use grayscale when the channel or layout calls for it.

How to use Grayscale

  1. Step 1: Upload a color photo.
  2. Step 2: The tool converts it to grayscale automatically in your browser.
  3. Step 3: Click Download to save the black-and-white version.

Practical tips

  • Keep a full-resolution master of every product photo; export sized copies for each sales channel.
  • For Meesho, aim for at least 600×600px and a manageable file size under 2MB.
  • For Amazon India main images, prefer accurate color on a white background and 1000px+ on the longest side.
  • This guide describes only the controls that exist on the tool page — no hidden brush tools or paid accounts.

Frequently asked questions

What does Grayscale do?

Grayscale is a free tool that removes color information and leaves luminance tones. Upload a color photo, convert, download. Local browser processing, no account.

Does grayscale shrink file size a lot?

Sometimes, especially after re-encoding as JPEG. For major size reductions, still use the Image Compressor after conversion.

Can I restore color later?

Not from the grayscale file. Keep the color original.
